About Kerdos Capital

Kerdos Capital is an independent quantitative investment research project. It builds systematic, rules-based models that decide how much a hypothetical portfolio should hold in US stocks, Bitcoin and other assets, and it publishes every call with the rules, the backtest and the limits behind it.

Independent research · not investment advice

Philosophy

Rules before opinions

  • Every exposure decision comes from written rules that read prices and published economic data after the close.
  • No discretionary overrides. News is shown for context and never changes a call.

Risk first

  • The models are built to avoid deep drawdowns, then to get back in quickly after a bottom.
  • A rule is kept only if it helps across different periods, not just in the years it was tuned on.

Show the work

  • Methods, factor weights and backtest assumptions are published on each desk page, including the ideas that were tested and rejected.
  • Backtests are labeled as backtests. Live results are tracked separately from the day a model goes live.

How it runs

Each desk updates every weekday before the US open using the previous session's closing prices, and Bitcoin updates daily. Data comes from Robinhood (prices, index levels, options open interest), FRED (rates, inflation, jobs) and Robert Shiller's dataset (long-run S&P history and valuation).

Every update is a public, timestamped commit in the site's GitHub history, so the record of what each model said, and when, cannot be rewritten after the fact.

The backtests start in 2003 for stocks and 2015 for Bitcoin. Their rules were chosen with that history in view, so they are in-sample: treat them as evidence of how the rules behave, not as a forecast of returns.

The name

Kerdos (κέρδος) is Greek for gain or profit. The aim is gain that survives the bad years: compounding by avoiding the large losses that take years to recover.

What Kerdos is not

Kerdos Capital is not a registered investment adviser or broker-dealer. It manages no money, takes no fees and sells no subscriptions. The model portfolios are hypothetical, their daily changes are not instructions to trade, and nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell any security or digital asset.
