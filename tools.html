03 · Historical replay

Replay a market

Pick a start date and play it forward to watch the desk react day by day. Before Oct 28, 2026 every day is a reconstructed backtest: today’s rules run over past data. From Oct 28, 2026 the calls were published and archived on the day.

Shading shows the state: champagne Risk On (100%), grey Balanced (50%), rust Risk Off (0%). The lower line is the score, with its exposure lines dashed.

04 · Exposure sandbox

Apply the regimes to a mix

Enter a hypothetical mix of stocks, Bitcoin and cash. See what the desks’ exposures would do to it today, and how the same mix would have behaved with and without them. An educational illustration, not a recommendation.

Both lines rebalance to the target mix every day, earn the 3-month T-bill rate on cash, and ignore trading costs and taxes. “With the regimes” scales the stock part by the stocks desk’s exposure and the Bitcoin part by the Bitcoin desk’s, acting at the next close, with the rest in T-bills. Important information. Kerdos Capital is an independent research project that publishes rules-based market models for education and discussion. It is not a registered investment adviser or broker-dealer. Nothing on this site is investment, tax or legal advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell any security or digital asset. The model portfolio is hypothetical: it manages no money, and its daily changes are not instructions to trade.

Hypothetical performance. Backtested results are hypothetical and are not the returns of any investor. They were computed with hindsight, from rules chosen with that history in view, and they leave out taxes and most real-world trading frictions. The flagship portfolio’s live record began at the open on Oct 26, 2026; every result before that date is a backtest. Past and backtested performance does not guarantee future results. Bitcoin and high-beta stocks are highly volatile, and any investment can lose money. Do your own research, or speak with a licensed professional, before investing.

Data and timing. Robinhood (prices, index levels, options open interest), FRED, Robert Shiller’s dataset, Coin Metrics, and the CME CF Bitcoin Real Time Index via Robinhood. Prices may be delayed or contain errors. Each model runs once a day on the latest completed session’s closing data, and this site refreshes once a day, by about 10:00 a.m. ET.
