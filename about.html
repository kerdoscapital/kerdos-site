About

Independent research, built in the open.

Kerdos Capital is an independent quantitative investment research project. It builds systematic, rules-based models that decide how much a hypothetical portfolio should hold in US stocks, Bitcoin and other assets, and it publishes every call with the rules, the backtest and the limits behind it.

Independent research · not investment advice

Transparency over promises.

Every call, rule and assumption is published, and every daily update is a timestamped public record. The work is judged on what it said and when, not on what it claims it will do.

Methodology over speculation.

Exposure comes from written rules that read prices and published economic data. A rule is kept only if it helps across different periods, not just in the years it was tuned on.

Discipline over emotion.

No discretionary overrides. Headlines are shown for context and never change a call, so the models act the same way in a calm market and a frightening one.

01Why Kerdos exists

Most market commentary is opinion delivered with confidence and checked by no one. Kerdos takes the other route: write the rules down, test them against history, publish them, and then let a public, timestamped record show how they behave from one day to the next.

The aim is not to predict every turn. It is to stay invested through healthy markets, step back when the evidence of risk builds, and get back in quickly after a bottom, with every decision visible and checkable.

02Why systematic

A systematic model makes the same decision from the same evidence every time. That consistency is the point: it removes the temptation to sell after a scare or chase after a rally, and it makes the approach testable, because a rule that is written down can be run over decades of data and measured.

The models are built risk-first. They are designed to avoid deep drawdowns, the losses that take years to recover, and their methods, factor weights and backtest assumptions are published on each desk page, including the ideas that were tested and rejected.

03Modeling, not speculating

Evidence, then exposure
Positions follow a score built from trend, momentum, market internals, macro data, valuation, volatility and calendar effects, never a hunch or a headline.
Honest about the past
Backtests are labeled as backtests and kept apart from the live record. Their rules were chosen with that history in view, so they are treated as evidence of how the rules behave, not as a forecast of returns.
Limits stated up front
Small samples, in-sample tuning and the outsized role of Bitcoin are spelled out on the methodology page, not buried.

04How it runs

Each model runs once a day on the latest completed session’s closing data; the Bitcoin desk also runs on weekends. This site refreshes once a day, by about 10:00 a.m. ET. Data comes from Robinhood (prices, index levels, options open interest), FRED (rates, inflation, jobs), Robert Shiller’s dataset (long-run S&P history and valuation) and Coin Metrics (Bitcoin history).

Every update is a public, timestamped commit in the site’s GitHub history, so the record of what each model said, and when, cannot be rewritten after the fact.

05Independent by design

Kerdos Capital is not a registered investment adviser or broker-dealer. It manages no money, takes no fees and sells no subscriptions, so there is nothing to sell and no reason to dress up a result. The model portfolios are hypothetical, their daily changes are not instructions to trade, and nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell any security or digital asset.

06The long view

The goal is a durable public research record: models that run every day, a live track record that lengthens with every session, and methods published in enough detail for anyone to check, reproduce or challenge. New desks are added only when they can meet the same standard of rules, testing and disclosure.

07The name

Kerdos (κέρδος) is Greek for gain or profit. The aim is gain that survives the bad years: compounding by avoiding the large losses that take years to recover.
