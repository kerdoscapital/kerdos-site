What the numbers on these cards mean
Regime and exposure
Each desk’s call (Bullish, Neutral, Correction, Bearish and so on) sets how much of its slice is invested: 100%, 50% or 0%, with the rest in T-bills.
Composite score
A weighted average of trend, momentum, market internals, macro, volatility and calendar signals, each scaled from −1 to +1. Above +0.15 is Bullish; below −0.10 is a Bearish signal.
Chance of a 10% drop in 6 months
How often SPY fell 10% or more within six months on past days with a similar score, since 2003.
Macro group vs the cash gate
A Bearish signal moves the stock desk to cash only when the macro signals are at or below −0.25. Above that it cuts to half (Correction).
Strength vs S&P 500
Whether the high-beta basket is keeping up with the S&P. Clearly lagging cuts that desk to half.
Put wall, call wall, dealer gamma
Where the most put and call open interest sits below and above the price, and an estimate of whether options dealers’ hedging tends to calm moves (positive) or amplify them (negative). Context only.

How exposure is set

US stocks

  • Bullish, Neutral: 100%. The composite score is positive or mixed.
  • Correction: 50%. The score turns Bearish but macro does not confirm it.
  • Bearish: 0%. The score is Bearish and macro confirms it. Cash earns T-bills.
  • Recovery: 100%. After a 20% decline SPY reclaims a rising 50-day average, or SPY rallies 10% off its 20-session low while inflation is under 4%.

Bitcoin

  • Bullish: 100%. The composite score is positive.
  • Neutral: 50%. Mixed signals.
  • Bearish: 0%. The score is negative. Cash earns T-bills.
  • Correction 50%, Recovery 100%. Bottoming rules that apply only once Bitcoin is 50% or more below its high.

High beta

  • S&P backdrop. Starts from the stock model: Bullish, Neutral and Recovery 100%, Correction 50%, Bearish 0%. The rules below let high beta cut on its own.
  • Lagging: 50%. High beta is clearly lagging the S&P 500 (basket vs SPY), even when the market model is Bullish.
  • Crash: 50%. The basket falls 12% or more in 10 sessions, whatever the market model says; back to the market call on a close above the basket’s close 5 sessions earlier.
  • Breakdown: 0%. The market model is Neutral or in Correction and the basket closes below its own 200-day average.
  • The basket. The 25 Nasdaq-100 stocks with the highest beta to QQQ over two years, equal weight, re-ranked each quarter.
  • Not in the model portfolio. A separate desk for a high-risk sleeve.
